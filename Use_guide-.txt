Quick Sanity Video Use guide-


MOBILE / TABLET CONTROLS:
- Use the bottom Preview, Timeline, Edit, and Project tabs.
- Swipe the empty timeline area to pan horizontally or vertically.
- Drag a clip body with one finger to move it in time or between layers.
- Drag the left or right clip edge to trim.
- Drag the ruler to scrub the playhead.
- Use Zoom - and Zoom + in the Timeline toolbar instead of a mouse wheel.
- Turn on Multi-select in Edit, then tap clips to build a selection.
- Use Clip Actions for copy, paste, split, magnet, fit, undo, redo, and delete.
- The Project tab contains save, load, export, canvas size, and help controls.
- Mobile Export As availability depends on the browser and is reported by the startup compatibility check and the Export As format menu.

Tip: Click the “three lines”(context menu) button; to hide this menu.

1.Load or Save a current project(always remember to save incrementally) will be downloaded to the downloads folder as a “Layercut” save. You may rename the file and it will still work.

2.When finished, select Export As. Choose:
- Video: browser-supported MOV, MP4, or WebM, resolution, 24/30/60 FPS, bitrate, optional audio, optional burned-in preview time, filename, and full/selected/custom range. MOV uses a genuine QuickTime `qt  ` container brand while the codec is selected by the browser.
- Animated GIF: full/selected/custom range, landscape/vertical/square/custom size, 5–20 FPS, 32–256 colors, optional dithering, looping, and burned-in preview time.
- Audio Only: uncompressed WAV or browser-supported M4A/MP4, WebM/Opus, or Ogg/Opus. WAV includes 44.1/48/96 kHz, 16-bit PCM, 24-bit PCM, 32-bit float, mono, and stereo controls.
- Current Frame Image: PNG, JPEG, or WebP at 50%, 100%, or 200% project size.
MOV/MP4/WebM video, compressed audio, and WAV export run in real time. GIF renders frame-by-frame. Keep the tab active and do not edit or scrub until export finishes. Unsupported formats are hidden automatically.

3.Set canvas size: 1280x720, 1920x1080, or 1024x576(more to come later).

4.Add, edit, name, select, toggle visibility, duplicate, or upload: image, audio or video clips to, Layers: from the layers 

5.Add, Edit, name, select(and multi-select), copy and paste, clips, to layer/s; by type: audio, video, text, transition, or scrub box to the selected clip/s

6.Add, Edit, multiple clip effects per clip type.

7.Click drag clips, shift click multiple clips and on the last selected clip click: start drag. If you drag after you selected all and released it will unselect the one you are trying to drag.

8.Grab playhead ruler to scrub timeline.

9.Hover the mouse over a specific spot, where you want to zoom into and use the scroll wheel to zoom in on that spot

10.Use middle mouse click to “grab” the canvas of the timeline and move yourself to where you want to look.

11.HOTKEYS:

Keyboard hotkeys:
Ctrl + Z → Undo
Ctrl + Y → Redo
Ctrl + Shift + Z → Redo
Ctrl + B → Split selected clip(s) at the playhead
Space → Play / Pause
Escape → Stop playback and close the clip context menu
Escape → Close the clip context menu

Modifier-assisted interactions
Shift + click on a clip → Multi-select / add clip to selection
Shift + drag-select behavior is preserved during clip/body/trim-handle selection logic.
Shift + right-click on empty timeline area → Opens the clip context menu without clearing the current selection
Right-click on empty timeline area without Shift → Clears selection, then opens the clip context menu


UI SCALING
- Use the top-right settings cog on desktop or the bottom-right Settings tab on mobile to scale the Top Toolbar, Project/File window, Clip Editor, Preview, Preview Info, and Timeline independently from 50% to 150%.
- Scaling preferences persist locally in the browser. Preview scaling does not change export resolution, and Timeline scaling does not change clip timing.
